06 · faq

%man eject

is this financial advice?

No. EJECT shows what dev and insider wallets do on-chain, the moment they do it. A dev selling isn't always a rug, and EJECT never tells you to buy anything.

is the auto-sell live?

The watch and the alarm are live today: free, in your browser, straight from Solana. Same-block auto-sell runs in preview. It shows you the block it would have sold your bag in and never touches your wallet.

who counts as an insider?

On the live log: wallets that bought in the first seconds of a launch. On your coins: any wallet you add, or tap + watch on the tape. If a dev hides behind fresh wallets, add them and they're watched too.

which coins work?

pump.fun coins on the bonding curve, read from the trade events Solana logs for every buy and sell. Coins that already migrated to PumpSwap are best-effort for now. EJECT watches from the moment you arm a coin. It doesn't replay the past.

do I need a wallet or keys?

No. Paste a coin, or a public address to load what you hold. Never paste a seed phrase or private key anywhere. EJECT will never ask for one.

will eject DM me?

Never. Anyone who does is not us.

No. EJECT shows what dev and insider wallets do on-chain, the moment they do it. A dev selling isn't always a rug, and EJECT never tells you to buy anything.

who counts as an insider?

On the live log: wallets that bought in the first seconds of a launch. On your coins: any wallet you add, or tap + watch on the tape. If a dev hides behind fresh wallets, add them and they're watched too.

No. Paste a coin, or a public address to load what you hold. Never paste a seed phrase or private key anywhere. EJECT will never ask for one.
